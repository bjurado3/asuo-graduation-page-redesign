Ready to graduate?

Explore this guide to ensure a seamless celebration of your life-changing accomplishment, whether you’re celebrating from home or attending the ceremonies in Arizona.

You’ll need to apply for graduation regardless of whether you’re attending ceremonies or not.

  1. 1

    Verify you’re ready to graduate. You can check with your academic advisor to confirm you’ve met your program’s requirements to graduate. You can also verify online:

    1. Undergraduate students use the Degree Audit Reporting System (DARS).
    2. Graduate students use the iPOS.
  2. 2

    Apply on My ASU. When you’re in My ASU, select Apply for Graduation within the My Programs box. Turn off any pop-up blockers before starting the process.

    Make sure to apply before these deadlines:

    • Fall: Oct. 1
    • Spring: Feb. 15
    • Summer: June 15
  3. 3

    Pay the fees.

    • Application fee: $100
    • Each additional, concurrent degree: $25
    • Late fee, if applicable: $35

    You’re set. Watch your inbox for multiple email communications about your upcoming graduation with information about important dates, ceremonies and how to prepare.

For undergraduates: If your GPA makes you eligible for honor cords, they will be distributed by mail at least one month before graduation.

Graduation is an exciting time. Take a look at these resources to help you plan your big moment.

Visiting ASU and attending your ceremony

Whether you already live near an ASU campus or are coming from out of town, here are some quick tips to help you prepare:

  • Graduates must wear caps, gowns and tassels to participate in their graduation ceremony. You can purchase your regalia online from Herff Jones, ASU’s official partner, or stop by during the online grad fair. Follow this guide to learn more about attire requirements and how to wear your cap and gown.
  • Coming from out of town? You can find hotel discounts through the ASU Alumni Association and the ASU Travel Guide. If you’re a student attending through a corporate partnership, you’ll be notified of additional lodging accommodations. Also, check out our coming to campus guide to learn about fun things to do and more.

Picking your ceremonies

We encourage all online students to attend their graduation ceremonies on campus to celebrate their accomplishments, but it is not a requirement to earn your degree and graduate. Check the graduation site for more details about upcoming ceremonies, the schedule and guest tickets.

You can attend commencement, college convocation and special-interest celebrations.

  • Commencement

    Commencement is the large university-wide ceremony. Your name will not be called and your guests will not need tickets for this event.

  • College convocation

    College convocation is a smaller ceremony where your name will be called on stage. You may need tickets for guests.

  • Special-interest celebrations

    Special-interest celebrations — such as the ASU Online Celebration that features lunch, photo opportunities, and the chance to connect with faculty, students, success coaches, and staff — are another type of ceremony you can attend. You may need tickets for guests.

Registering for a ceremony

After applying for graduation and paying the fees, go to My ASU and select the Register Ceremonies link in the graduation tab. From there, fill out the form for each ceremony you’d like to attend. A confirmation email will be sent after completing each form.

Please note:

  • Spring ceremony registration usually opens in late February and closes around the first week of April.
  • Fall ceremony registration opens in September and often closes in November.

Summer graduates

Graduates who are within 12 credits or less from program completion and are completing coursework in the summer may participate in ceremonies in May, or in December following course completion. This applies to undergraduate candidates and most master’s degree candidates. Check with your success coach for details.

We’ve broken down the differences between a commencement and a convocation here:

  • Commencement:
    • These are large university-wide ceremonies for undergraduate and graduate students where degrees are conferred by the President of ASU.
    • An unlimited number of guests are permitted.
    • Bachelor's degree candidates will be recognized as a group. Individual names will not be called and students do not walk across the stage.
    • Master's degree candidates will be recognized as a group. Doctoral degree candidates will be hooded by their selected faculty and individual names will be called to cross the stage.
  • Convocation:
    • These are smaller, college-level ceremonies. These might not take place on the same day as commencement.
    • Some convocation ceremonies require tickets.
    • Each graduate’s name will be individually called and they will cross the stage at convocation ceremonies, including each college convocation as well as the specialty convocations.

Honor cords are only for undergraduate students.

You’ll automatically have your honor cords mailed to you if your GPA is eligible. Here are the requirements breakdowns:

  • 3.40-3.59 - cum laude
  • 3.60-3.79 - magna cum laude
  • 3.80-4.00 - summa cum laude
