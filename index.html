Picking your ceremonies

We encourage all our online students to attend their graduation ceremonies on campus to celebrate their accomplishments, but it is not a requirement to earn your degree and graduate.

You have the following ceremony types you can register for:

Commencement is the large university-wide ceremony. Your name will not be called and your guests will not need tickets for this event.

College convocation is a smaller ceremony where your name will be called on stage. You may need tickets for guests.

Special interest celebrations, such as the ASU Online Celebration that features lunch, fun and professional photo opportunities and the chance to connect with faculty, students, success coaches and staff, are another type of ceremony you can attend where your name is called. You may need tickets for guests.
